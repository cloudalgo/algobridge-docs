# Pricing: AlgoBridge

AlgoBridge is free to use. Every feature is in the free release. There is no per-row, per-record or per-user charge.

## Free (self-hosted)
- Price: $0
- Licence: free of charge under CloudAlgo's licence terms; not open source
- Registration key: free, one per instance, matching the org kind. Production orgs request their own key; sandboxes and Developer Edition orgs use a shared key. Verified inside the instance; nothing is sent to CloudAlgo in this version.
- Instances: as many as you run; each syncs one Salesforce org with one PostgreSQL database
- You provide: a container runtime on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud (or their managed Kubernetes) or Heroku, PostgreSQL 15 or later, outbound HTTPS
- You pay for: your own infrastructure, and the Salesforce API calls AlgoBridge makes from your org's existing daily allowance

## Paid help from CloudAlgo (optional)
- Price: quoted per engagement; contact CloudAlgo
- Moving from your current sync tool, mapping and schema review, production support with response-time commitments
- Book: https://cloudalgo.com/#schedule
